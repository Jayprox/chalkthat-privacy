Frequently Asked Questions

I'm having trouble signing in. What should I do?

Double-check your username and password. If you still can't sign in, email us at the address above and we'll help sort it out.

Does Chalk That NFL let me place bets or wagers?

No. Chalk That NFL is a research and analytics tool for team, player, and matchup information. It does not process wagers or handle real-money transactions of any kind.

How do I request that my account or data be deleted?

Email us at the address above with your account details and we'll process the deletion request.

I found a bug or have a feature request. Where do I send it?

We'd love to hear about it — email us with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead), including screenshots if possible.
