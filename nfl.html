Chalk That NFL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Chalk That NFL ("the App") is a research and analytics tool for NFL team, player, and matchup information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects only the information needed to operate your account:

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account.
  • User ID / account identifier — an internal identifier used to associate your data with your account.

The App does not access your camera, photo library, contacts, location, or health data, and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. The App does not request Sign in with Apple or any third-party login.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To associate your saved data and preferences with your account.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the App and its backend services.

3. NFL Stats, Matchups & Market Data

The App displays NFL team, player, matchup, and market-context information sourced from our backend and third-party sports-data providers. These requests do not include any of your personal information — data is fetched generically and displayed to you within the App.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Information is stored on our backend servers solely to operate the App.

5. Data Storage & Security

All communication between the App and our backend is encrypted via HTTPS. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

6. Data Retention & Account Management

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, please contact us using the information below.

7. No Real-Money Transactions

Chalk That NFL is a research and analytics tool. It does not process wagers, bets, or real-money transactions of any kind.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
